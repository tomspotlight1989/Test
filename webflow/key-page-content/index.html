Key Page Content Optimisation

Strong content is essential to the organic performance of your product and service pages.

The challenge

Even amazing businesses struggle for organic visibility when the content on their key pages isn't aligned with what searchers and search engines want to see.

Our approach

Generic SEO firms hand over keyword recommendations and leave your team to action them. We deliver clear, ready to publish content updates.

Our delivery

Our key page content optimisation service provides a full content rewrite for selected key product and service pages on your website, as well as any other pages whose content requires amending to improve the performance of those selected pages.

Our experts use the following data led approach
Search intent alignment

We review the page against the intent of its highest priority keyword group, and realign the content so it matches what searchers are actually looking for.

Header structure and keyword grouping

We review your header structure and align it to the natural groupings of secondary keywords that we identify for the service.

Content gaps

We identify the questions your customers are asking that the page doesn't currently answer, and provide additional content to close the gaps.

Content authority and trust signals

Where relevant, we recommend including reliable data and evidence that strengthens the page's credibility with both readers and search engines.

Cannibalisation and duplication

We identify where other pages are competing for the same search terms, or share duplicated headers and content, and resolve any resulting confusion.
